# Brand

The code of Holy PDF is free software. The brand is a trademark matter. This page draws the line.

## What the brand is

- the name **Holy PDF**, and any name that could be confused with it;
- the logo: the halo on a yellow disc (`apps/web/public/favicon.svg`, `apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons/`);
- the monk character in a brown habit with a yellow cord, in all his moods, and the illustrations of the site and of the desktop app that feature him (`apps/web/src/illustrations/Monk.tsx`, `Scene.tsx`, `Avatar.tsx`), as well as the films in `apps/web/public/videos/`;
- the domain `holy-pdf.com` (not registered yet) and the project's social accounts.

Snouzylabs S.R.L., the publisher of Holy PDF, claims the name and the monk as its marks. A trademark filing is planned; until it is granted, the name is an unregistered mark.

## How this fits with the AGPL

The source files of the logo and the illustrations are part of the program and ship under the AGPL like the rest, so that anyone can build and run the site. That license gives you copyright permissions. It gives you no right to use these signs to say that something is Holy PDF, or comes from Holy PDF, when it does not: [LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md](LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md) declines trademark rights, as section 7(e) of the AGPL allows.

## What you may do

- Say that your project uses or is based on Holy PDF's code, and link here.
- Keep the name where the license requires it: copyright notices, license texts, git history.
- Show screenshots of Holy PDF, and use the press kit, in articles, reviews, talks and courses.
- Package the unmodified software for a distribution or a package manager under the name Holy PDF, as long as it stays unmodified and points back here.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Distribute a fork, a modified build or a hosted copy under the name Holy PDF or a name that could be confused with it.
- Use the logo, the monk or the illustrations to identify your own product, site or service, or in its marketing.
- Present your version as official, or as endorsed by, affiliated with or supported by Holy PDF.
- Register domains, handles or app-store listings that use the name.

## If you fork

Pick your own name, replace the logo and the monk wherever a visitor sees them, and remove the name from the user-facing text (`apps/web/src/i18n/`, the content pages, the metadata, and in the desktop app `apps/desktop/app/texts.ts`, the menus and the product name in `apps/desktop/src-tauri/`). Leave the license notices untouched. The AGPL asks that your users can get your source; the brand asks that they can tell your project from this one.

Permission requests: open an issue on GitHub. An email address will come with the site's launch.
