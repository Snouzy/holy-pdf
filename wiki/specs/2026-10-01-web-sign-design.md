# Web: Sign a PDF

## Scope

Add a visual signature: drawn freehand, typed as text or imported from a PNG/JPG/JPEG. One PDF at a time, several placements possible, on any page. Each placement can be moved, resized proportionally, rotated freely or removed. A small square handle at the corner resizes proportionally and keeps the opposite corner fixed; below the signature (above it near the bottom of the page), a four-way arrow moves it by drag and by the keyboard arrows, next to the rotate and delete buttons. The three tabs Draw, Text and Import an image keep the same size from one mode to the next: the panel reserves the space of its scrollbar. A click outside or Escape hides the frame and the controls, with no change to the signature; a click on the signature selects it again. The selection controls in the panel stay usable. Shift rounds the rotation to steps of 15°, the keyboard arrows adjust it by 1° (15° with Shift). The corners, as actually rotated, stay inside the page. A resize with the keyboard or with the panel slider keeps the center; the rectangle before rotation can go beyond the 0–1 coordinates if its transformed corners stay inside. No account, no upload, no storage of the signature after the page closes. This is not a digital signature with a certificate.

## Flow and identity

While the file opens, a static skeleton sheet replaces the old tab and its animated bar. The gray lines announce the document; the same skeleton stays in the sheet while the preview renders. The name and the remove control stay available above it while the file opens. Errors and the password use a simple block, without a tab. No additional engine, request or continuous animation.

Brother Quill welcomes the file. Only one page of the PDF shows at a time, with previous/next and the page number in a floating bar at the bottom of the PDF area. This bar stays reachable during scroll and stops with the document; on mobile, it sits above the final action. The ready document shows no file label. The bar also offers a zoom from 50 to 200%, in steps of 25%, and a reset to 100%. The zoom changes only the display size, without a new PDF render; the placements keep their normalized coordinates. A control rotates the page by 90° on each click; the exported PDF keeps this rotation, also on a page without a signature. The existing right panel contains Draw / Import an image, the creation of the signature and the final action. On phones, keep a readable flow and controls of 44 px. The drawing stays dark on a white background, also in dark mode; its output has a transparent background. A PNG keeps its transparency. A JPG keeps its background: no implicit automatic background removal.

Existing palette: background #EEF1F6, ink #141A2E, action #2346D8, Edit accent #B4418E, yellow #FFE45C. Headings in Bricolage Grotesque, text in Figtree. Reuse the persistent panel and the navigation border; no new interface library.

The "Add to this page" action stays visible in the panel footer, above "Sign the PDF", outside the scrolling options and without covering the preview. The text is ready automatically after typing; the drawing is prepared at the end of the gesture. No intermediate "Use" step. Each mode keeps its draft; an empty input or a switch to an empty mode disables the add action, but does not erase the elements already placed and does not prevent their export.

With a ready draft and no selection, a click in the page offers "Add here". The mark is centered on the chosen point, within the page limits, with the zoom and the rotations taken into account. The first click outside a selected mark only deselects it. A pointer move during the gesture does not offer an add.

## Engine and preservation

### Text added on 2 October

The Text mode accepts one line of 120 characters at most: a note, initials or a name. Two styles, Handwritten (Caveat, self-hosted under the OFL license) and Simple (the existing font of the site; if it could not load, the fallback font of the page, without blocking). The handwritten font loads only when this mode opens; the render waits for the font before any conversion. Prepare the text as a transparent image, then place it and handle it with the existing controls. Several different texts and signatures can coexist; a new element does not delete the ones already placed. Each resource stays shared between its placements in the PDF. The added text is a visual mark, not selectable PDF text; the original text of the document stays unchanged. No persistent storage and no external service.

Add the images on a second handle of the original document, never in a copy of its pages. For each different image or text, a temporary page of 1 × 1 unit holds the mark and becomes a shared Form XObject: all placements of that mark reuse its image and its mask. Coordinates are fractions of the page from the top left corner of the displayed page; one central conversion takes CropBox and rotation into account, with `FPDF_DeviceToPage`. Do not modify the open document: a return to the settings and a new export do not stack the signatures. The coordinates of the placements stay in the original display frame; the rotation of their image is computed around its center in physical units, then the page rotation deltas are applied after insertion. Refuse explicitly the documents that are already digitally signed, so that a certificate is not invalidated silently. Password protection follows the existing warning of the tool.

## Performance

Editor loaded only after a PDF opens in Sign, including its texts and styles. No new engine or dependency; use the PDFium that is already present. One page preview at a time, width capped at 1,200 px, side at 1,600 px and area at 1.5 Mpx; no rasterization of the exported document. Signature normalized to 1,600 px on a side at most and 1 Mpx. PNG/JPEG import capped at 10 MiB and 16 Mpx before decoding, with explicit messages for overflow/failure. Release Blob URLs and bitmaps. During the drawing, smooth the mouse points moderately and add only the new quadratic Bézier segment directly in the canvas; get the coalesced events when they are available, with a fallback. The enlarged area and Undo last stroke share a bounded history of points; a full replay is only for these actions, never for a pointer move. The last point at release and the isolated points stay kept; during a move, never compute a PDF render again. Initial JavaScript budgets kept: home 24 KiB, tools 50 KiB. Measure open/export time and move cost on a synthetic fixture, without turning these measures into a universal guarantee.

Each different mark is encoded only once in the PDF, even with several placements. The referenced images are capped together at 16 Mpx (64 MB of RGBA pixels); this budget does not count as the total memory of the engine. Unused resources are released and are not sent to the Worker. An import in progress blocks the export of the old signature. A PNG/JPG drop on its importer also closes the global drop overlay, without a replacement of the PDF. After an add, the placement gets the focus and becomes visible, in particular on phones.

## Validation

Engine tests: placement after rotation and crop, transparency, text and structures kept, source intact and repeated exports, already signed PDF refused. Browser tests: drawing, PNG/JPG/JPEG import, placement and size, page change, removal, real export, phone, import failure. Types, unit tests, build, SEO and Lighthouse budgets. Document the results and the limits.

## Drawing comfort

Enlarged area in a native dialog, Undo last stroke, canvas of 1,280 × 480 pixels on each surface and a history capped at 12,000 points. The white belongs to the interface; the signature raster keeps its transparency. The size handle follows the rotated corner; the rotate button stays upright and away from the stroke, also after a rotation of the page.

References reviewed: [feedback on mouse jitter](https://github.com/szimek/signature_pad/issues/183), [trade-off between filtering and lag](https://github.com/szimek/signature_pad/issues/251), [coalesced pointer events](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/PointerEvent/getCoalescedEvents). The local implementation adds one segment at a time and stays in the module loaded on demand.
