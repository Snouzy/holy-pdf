faq:
  - question: "Can the hidden text be recovered?"

  - question: "Is my PDF sent to a server?"
    answer: "No. Everything happens in your browser, on your device. No file is sent or stored."
  - question: "What happens to the rest of the redacted page?"
    answer: "It stays visible, as it was, but as a picture: it can no longer be selected or searched. The other pages do not change."
  - question: "Do bookmarks and links still work?"
    answer: "Yes: those that led to a redacted page still lead there. However, the document's title, bookmarks and metadata are not checked: make sure they hold nothing secret."
  - question: "Will the file be heavier?"
    answer: "A little: a redacted page is a picture of about 1 MB at A4 size."
