  - "Take photos of your pages, or drop the photos in the area above: JPEG, PNG or iPhone HEIC."

faq:
  - question: "Are my photos sent to a server?"
    answer: "No. The photos are processed in your browser, on your device. No photo is sent or stored, and a photo's GPS position never enters the PDF."
  - question: "How is the page found?"
    answer: "Brother Snap looks for the sheet's outline, then fits each edge onto the paper's edge, even when a corner is hidden. When in doubt, he marks the page ⚠ for you to check."
  - question: "What does the Document rendering do?"
    answer: "It whitens the paper, removes the shadows and keeps the ink black, like a scanner. The Color rendering keeps the colours, for a certificate with a security background or a photo."
  - question: "Which size is the PDF?"
    answer: "A4 when the page has its shape, otherwise the size measured at 200 dpi. You can choose A4, A5 or Letter for each page."
  - question: "Can I split the pages into several documents?"
    answer: "Yes: drag a page onto another document, or onto the “New document” box. Each document becomes its own PDF."
---

Scanning a document lets you send a signed paper, an invoice or an ID without a scanner: a photo is enough.

Brother Snap does everything on your device: your photos never leave your computer or your phone.
