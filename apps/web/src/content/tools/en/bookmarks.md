faq:
  - question: "What is a bookmark for?"
    answer: "A bookmark leads to a page in one click. PDF readers show the bookmarks in their sidebar, like a table of contents: useful for a report, a course or a book."
  - question: "How do I put a bookmark under another?"
    answer: "The right arrow puts a bookmark under the one above, the left arrow moves it up one level. A new bookmark places itself after those of its page and of the pages before."
  - question: "Are the bookmarks my PDF already has kept?"
    answer: "Yes, with their title, their page and the spot they lead to. A bookmark that opens a web address or leads to no page is not kept, and the screen says how many."
  - question: "Is my PDF sent to a server?"
    answer: "No. Everything happens in your browser, on your device. No file is sent or stored."
  - question: "What about a protected or signed PDF?"
    answer: "A protected PDF opens with its password, and the copy keeps that password. A signed PDF is refused: new bookmarks would make its signature invalid."

Adding bookmarks to a PDF gives the reader a clickable outline: each chapter is one click away, without scrolling through the whole document.

Brother Ribbon does everything on your device: your documents never leave your computer or your phone.
