faq:
  - question: "Why pixelize a PDF?"
    answer: "To share a document that can be read and printed, but whose text does not copy easily: each page becomes a picture."
  - question: "Is my PDF sent to a server?"
    answer: "No. The pages are converted in your browser, on your device. No file is sent or stored."
  - question: "Does search in the PDF still work?"
    answer: "No: the pages hold no text anymore, only pictures. Keep the original if you need to search it."
  - question: "Will the file be heavier?"
    answer: "Often, for a PDF made mostly of text. The normal resolution (150 ppi) keeps the file light; the high one (300 ppi) prints sharper."
  - question: "Is it real protection?"
    answer: "No. A picture can still be read by text recognition software. Pixelizing makes copying harder, without preventing it."

Pixelizing a PDF lets you send a frozen version of a document: reading and printing it stays easy, copying its text does not.

Brother Glass does everything on your device: your documents never leave your computer or your phone.
