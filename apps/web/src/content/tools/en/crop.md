    answer: "The frame keeps the same share of each page: a bigger page loses wider margins, in the same proportions."
  - question: "Can I crop a single page?"

  - question: "What about a protected or signed PDF?"

Cropping a PDF removes the margins of a scan, isolates a table or a picture, or brings a page down to the size worth reading on a screen.

Brother Framer does everything on your device: your documents never leave your computer or your phone.
