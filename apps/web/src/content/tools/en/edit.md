steps:
  - "Drop your PDF in the area above: its first page shows."
  - "Pick a tool, then click or drag on the page: text, image, rectangle, arrow, pen, highlighter, note, word highlight, link or stamp. A form's fields are filled in place: click into one. The Field tool adds some: text, checkbox or dropdown."
  - "Correct, move or delete what the PDF already holds; move, resize or delete what you added; then click “Save the changes”."
faq:
  - question: "Can I change the text already in the PDF?"
    answer: "Yes: double-click a line to correct it in place. It keeps its font when that font can write the letters you type; otherwise it switches to the closest standard font, and the screen tells you. A line can also be moved, or deleted."
  - question: "Can I annotate the PDF, and add links?"
    answer: "Yes: a note with its text and author, a highlight, an underline or a strike-through on the words you drag over, and a link to a web address or to a page of the document. They are real annotations: Acrobat and Preview open them."
  - question: "Can I move, crop or delete an image of the PDF?"
    answer: "Yes: click it, then drag it, resize it by its corners, turn it, mirror it, crop it or delete it. A drawing moves and deletes too. A deleted object leaves the file."
  - question: "Does the text wrap?"
    answer: "Yes: drag with the Text tool to draw a box, and your lines wrap at its width, on screen as in the file. A single click places a free text."
  - question: "Does the added text stay text?"
    answer: "Yes: it can be selected, copied and found by search, in every PDF reader."
  - question: "Which letters can I write?"
    answer: "Latin letters with their common accents (é, è, ç, ü…). The PDF's standard fonts cannot write some Eastern European letters, emojis or other alphabets: the screen says so."
  - question: "Is my PDF sent to a server?"
    answer: "No. Everything happens in your browser, on your device. No file is sent or stored."
  - question: "What about a protected or signed PDF?"
    answer: "A protected PDF opens with its password, and the copy keeps it. A signed PDF is refused: the edited copy would make its signature invalid."
---

Editing a PDF lets you correct a date or a name, fill in a printed form, annotate a document, circle a detail or add a logo, without installing anything.

Brother Scribe does everything on your device: your documents never leave your computer or your phone.
