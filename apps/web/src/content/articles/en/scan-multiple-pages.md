You do not need a desktop scanner to combine several pages into a PDF. Your phone is enough: photograph the pages, then let Holy PDF straighten and group them on your device.

## 1. Photograph every page

Place the sheets flat under even light. Keep the whole page inside the frame and avoid casting a shadow with your hand or phone.

Take all the photos before you start. You can then select them together in [Scan a document](/en/scanner). The tool accepts JPEG and PNG files, as well as HEIC photos from an iPhone.

## 2. Import the photos together

Open the Scanner and choose every page of the document in the same import. They are placed in one multi-page document.

Holy PDF detects the paper, straightens the page, and removes shadows. Check any page marked with a warning: move its corners if the outline is wrong, then erase anything outside the paper when necessary.

All this processing happens in your browser. The photos are not uploaded to a server.

## 3. Put the pages in order

Drag the pages into the correct order. You can also:

- rotate a page photographed upside down;
- remove a duplicate page;
- move a page to another document;
- create a new document if you photographed several sets of paperwork together.

If you need to fix the order later, use [Organize PDF pages](/en/organize-pdf).

## 4. Make the PDF searchable

The **Searchable text** option adds an invisible text layer over the pages. You can then find a word or copy a passage without changing how the scan looks.

Text recognition currently supports English and French. To process a PDF that has already been scanned, use [PDF OCR](/en/ocr-pdf) instead.

## 5. Download one PDF

Give the document a clear name, then download it. Every page in that document is saved in one PDF.

Open the file before sending it and check:

- every page is present;
- the pages are in the right order;
- the text is readable;
- no edge has been cut off;
- the file meets the recipient's size limit.

If the file is too large, run it through [Compress PDF](/en/compress-pdf).

## If your pages are already separate PDFs

You do not need to photograph them again. Open [Merge PDF files](/en/merge-pdf), add the files, arrange them, and download the final document.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I scan two pages into the same PDF?

Yes. Select both photos in the same import and they will become the two pages of one document.

### Can I use HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Yes. The Scanner accepts HEIC photos as well as JPEG and PNG files.

### How do I separate two documents photographed together?

Move the pages of the second document to the “New document” area. You can then download each document separately.

### Are my photos uploaded to the internet?

No. Holy PDF processes the photos in your browser. They stay on your device.

### Can I add more pages after the first import?

Yes. Add the new photos, move them into the right document, and put them in order.
