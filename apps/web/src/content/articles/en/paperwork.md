An office asks you for an application file: ID, proof of address, tax notice. You have photos taken with your phone, a few PDFs, and a size limit to respect. Here is how to turn them into one clean PDF. Your papers leave your device only when you submit the final file.

## 1. Turn the photos into a PDF

Open [Convert JPG to PDF](/en/jpg-to-pdf) and drop your photos: each photo becomes a page. Photograph the papers flat, well lit, without the shadow of your hand or phone. The tool takes JPG and PNG photos. An iPhone often saves HEIC: on a Mac, open the photo in Preview and export it as JPEG, or choose "Most Compatible" in the iPhone's Camera settings.

## 2. Combine the documents

Open [Merge PDF files](/en/merge-pdf) and drop every PDF of the application, including the one made from the photos. Put the files in the order asked for.

## 3. Put the pages in order

A page upside down: [Rotate a PDF](/en/rotate-pdf). A page too many: [Delete pages from a PDF](/en/delete-pdf-pages). To rearrange everything: [Organize PDF pages](/en/organize-pdf).

## 4. Make the file lighter

The upload site refuses files that are too heavy? Open [Compress a PDF](/en/compress-pdf). The "Recommended" level is usually enough: the file gets lighter, and the photos stay sharp.

## Check before you send

Open the final PDF and leaf through it: every page, in the right order, readable. Give it a clear name, such as `housing-application-smith.pdf`. Keep your original files.
