## No cookies

Holy PDF sets no cookies. The site has no audience measurement tool, no advertising and no tracking pixel. So there is no banner to accept.

## Two settings, on your device

The site keeps two settings in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`), and only if you choose them:

| Key | What it keeps | When it is created |
|---|---|---|
| `theme` | the light or dark theme | when you press the theme button |
| `view` | the compact view of the home page | when you press "Compact view" |

These settings stay on your device and are never sent. They serve a customisation you asked for: under the recommendations of the CNIL, the French data protection authority, they need no consent.

## Clearing them

In your browser settings, clear the data of the site. Holy PDF then goes back to your device's theme and to the cards of the home page.

## If this changes

If the site ever adds a measurement tool, this page will change first, and your consent will be asked where the law requires it.
