<h2 id="mac">Holy PDF for Mac and Windows, then Linux</h2>

A desktop app is in the works, for Mac and Windows, then Linux. It is built on the site's code: the same tools, the same monks, the same engine. And the same promise: your files stay on your device.

It will bring what the site cannot: open a PDF with a double click, process a whole folder, save in place, work offline.

The site's code is free software, published under the AGPL-3.0-or-later licence on [GitHub](https://github.com/Snouzy/holy-pdf). The app will live in the same repository.

<h2 id="iphone">Holy PDF for iPhone</h2>

An iPhone app will come after the desktop one, with the camera for the Scanner. Until then, the site works in Safari on the iPhone, [Scanner](/en/scanner) included.
