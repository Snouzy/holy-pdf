## Is Holy PDF free?

Yes. The tools of the site are free, with no hidden limit and no watermark.

## Do I need an account?

No. Open a tool, drop your files, get the result.

## Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. They are processed in your browser, on your device, and never leave it. [How it works, and how to check it](/en/blog/your-pdfs-stay-on-your-device).

## Which browsers?

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on a computer as on a phone. The site needs JavaScript and WebAssembly, which all these browsers offer.

## Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in the phone's browser, with no app to install. With a very large file, a phone can be slower than a computer: all the work happens on the device.

## Is there a maximum size?

The site sets no size limit. The engine works in the browser's memory, which has its own limit: a very large file can fail, especially on a phone. On a recent computer, merging ten files of 50 pages takes less than two seconds.

## Is a signature made with Holy PDF legally valid?

Sign places an image of your signature in the PDF: a simple electronic signature. It holds for everyday documents, when the parties accept it. It is not a qualified signature under the eIDAS regulation, and it does not replace one where the law requires it.

## Is there an app?

Not yet. A desktop app for Mac and Windows, then Linux, is in the works, built on the site's code, with the same promise: your files stay on your device. See [the apps](/en/apps).

## Is the code of Holy PDF open?

Yes. The site's code is published under the AGPL-3.0-or-later licence on [GitHub](https://github.com/Snouzy/holy-pdf). You can also check the promise without reading a line of code. Open the Network tab of your browser's developer tools, run a tool, and watch: no request carries your file.
