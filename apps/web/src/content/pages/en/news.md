## October 5, 2026

The code of the site is published as free software under the AGPL-3.0-or-later licence, on [GitHub](https://github.com/Snouzy/holy-pdf). You can read it, change it, and check for yourself that your files never leave your device.

## October 4, 2026

Two monks join the monastery:

- [Edit a PDF](/en/edit-pdf): Brother Scribe adds text, images, shapes, annotations, links and stamps, fills in forms and creates their fields.
- [Crop a PDF](/en/crop-pdf): Brother Framer removes the margins, on one page or all of them.

## October 3, 2026

Four monks join the monastery:

- [Add bookmarks to a PDF](/en/pdf-bookmarks), with Brother Ribbon;
- [Overlay two PDFs](/en/overlay-pdf), with Brother Layer;
- [Repair a PDF](/en/repair-pdf), with Brother Mender;
- [Scan a document](/en/scanner), with Brother Snap.

## October 2, 2026

Twelve monks join the monastery:

- [Sign a PDF](/en/sign-pdf), with Brother Quill;
- [Add a watermark to a PDF](/en/watermark-pdf), with Brother Stamp;
- [Add page numbers to a PDF](/en/page-numbers-pdf), with Brother Folio;
- [Put several pages on one sheet](/en/pages-per-sheet), with Brother Mosaic;
- [Split the pages of a PDF in half](/en/split-pages-in-half), with Brother Trimmer;
- [Pixelize a PDF](/en/pixelize-pdf), with Brother Glass;
- [Flatten a PDF](/en/flatten-pdf), with Brother Roller;
- [Protect a PDF with a password](/en/protect-pdf), with Brother Padlock;
- [Unlock a PDF](/en/unlock-pdf), with Brother Passkey;
- [Redact a PDF](/en/redact-pdf), with Brother Inkpot;
- [OCR: make a PDF searchable](/en/ocr-pdf), with Brother Reader;
- [Convert a PDF to Word](/en/pdf-to-word), with Brother Copyist.

## October 1, 2026

Two monks join the monastery:

- [Compress a PDF](/en/compress-pdf): Brother Press makes the photos of a PDF lighter, without touching the text.
- [Convert PDF to JPG](/en/pdf-to-jpg): Brother Illuminator turns each page into an image, or gets the photos out of the PDF.

- [Organize PDF pages](/en/organize-pdf), with Brother Binder;
- [Delete pages from a PDF](/en/delete-pdf-pages), with Brother Eraser;
- [Extract pages from a PDF](/en/extract-pdf-pages), with Brother Lens;
- [Rotate a PDF](/en/rotate-pdf), with Brother Spin;
- [Convert JPG to PDF](/en/jpg-to-pdf), with Brother Frame.
